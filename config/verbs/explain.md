In three or four lines, tell me what you are doing right now and why.

I am looking at a panel of agents and cannot tell what this one is up to. I
want to re-enter your head quickly, not read a report.

- What you are working on **at this moment** — the immediate thing, not the
  whole task.
- Why that is the next step, in one clause.
- Where you are in the larger job: roughly how far through, and what is left.
- Anything you are stuck on, waiting for, or unsure about.

Write it as though I have been away for twenty minutes and just walked back
to your screen. No preamble, no restating the original request back to me, no
summary of what you have already told me before.

If you are between things and genuinely doing nothing, say that plainly. "I
finished the migration and I am waiting for you to tell me whether to run it"
is a perfect answer.

Then carry on with what you were doing. This is a glance, not an interruption
— do not stop, and do not treat it as a request to change course.
